# Donut Lab — The Complete Investigation

**Last Updated:** June 16, 2026  
**Status:** Active criminal investigation | Credibility: Collapsed

---

## Executive Summary

Donut Lab, a Finnish startup led by CEO **Marko Lehtimäki**, announced a "production-ready all-solid-state battery" at **CES 2026** claiming:
- **400 Wh/kg** energy density
- **100,000 charge cycles**
- **5-minute full charge**

In April 2026, a whistleblower — former Nordic Nano CCO **Lauri Peltola** — filed a **criminal complaint** in Finland alleging the specs were fabricated. By June 2026, a comprehensive investigation by YouTube channel **Ziroth** (consulting 20+ independent battery experts including Fraunhofer Institute researchers) produced definitive electrochemical proof that the battery is a **conventional NCM lithium-ion pouch cell**, not solid-state. Real energy density: ~298 Wh/kg.

The company raised **~$25 million** from over **1,300 mostly small retail investors** (many via Finland's Springvest crowdfunding platform), inflated its valuation to **$1.25 billion** post-CES, and CEO Marko Lehtimäki promised investors "up to 10x return in 12–18 months."

Finnish **financial and criminal authorities** are reportedly investigating.

## 3. The Scientific Debunking — Irrefutable Proof

### Evidence #1: Voltage Curves

| Metric | Donut Lab Claim | Actual Measurement | What It Means |
|--------|----------------|-------------------|---------------|
| Chemistry | Sodium-ion solid-state | NCM lithium-ion | Fraudulent claim |
| Voltage at 50% SoC | N/A (claimed sodium-ion) | **3.7–3.8 V** | Matches NCM Li-ion exactly |
| Sodium-ion theoretical max at 50% SoC | — | ~3.5 V | Donut Lab cell exceeds this |

> *"The cell sits at 3.7–3.8 volts at 50% state of charge — right where lithium-ion cells operate. Sodium-ion cells don't go significantly past 3.5 volts at 50% SOC."* — Ziroth investigation

### Evidence #2: Anode Expansion "Kink"

Graphite anodes in lithium-ion cells produce a **distinctive expansion kink** at 50–70% state of charge, caused by lithium ions ordering within graphite's layered structure. The Donut Lab cell shows **exactly that kink**.

> *"Sodium ions are physically too large to intercalate into graphite. The presence of this graphite signature proves the cell uses lithium ions. It's like we have a slightly noisy fingerprint and a picture of the suspect's face. And yet again, it's a match."*

### Evidence #3: Visible Swelling After 4 Cycles

VTT's own tests showed the cell **visibly swelling** after just 4 charge cycles. Liquid electrolytes expand during charging — solid-state electrolytes do not.

### Evidence #4: Real Energy Density

Calculated from Ziroth's electrochemical data: **~298 Wh/kg** — respectable for a lithium-ion cell, but far from the claimed 400 Wh/kg.

### Evidence #5: VTT's Selective Testing

Finland's **VTT Technical Research Centre** — a highly reputable national lab — was paid to run tests. But critically, VTT:

- ✅ Confirmed **fast charging** and **high-temperature survival** (peripheral claims)
- ❌ **Never tested** energy density or cycle life
- ❌ Showed visible swelling after 4 cycles (documented but not highlighted)

> *"It seems clear that Donut Lab's CEO Marko Lehtimäki engaged in 'authority laundering' by paying Finland's highly reputable VTT Technical Research Centre to run certain tests — and only those tests — to create the illusion of legitimacy."* — The Verge

## 5. The Whistleblower — Lauri Peltola

- **Role:** Former Chief Commercial Officer (CCO) of Nordic Nano
- **Action:** Filed **criminal complaint** in Finland (April 2026)
- **Allegations:**
  - Exaggerated energy density and lifespan metrics
  - Questionable production scalability
  - Inconsistencies between public statements and actual development stage
  - Battery model shown in demos was an **abandoned first-generation design** from CT Coatings — CT Coatings had already moved to a newer, early-stage design

### Responses to Whistleblower

- **Marko Lehtimäki (Donut Lab CEO):** Claimed no knowledge of Peltola's complaint
- **Esa Parjanen (Nordic Nano CEO):** Denied accusations, said Peltola's views were not company's, and Peltola was **not involved with Nordic's battery project**
- Both companies question whistleblower credibility, suggesting "misunderstanding of the technology"

## 6. The Pre-Production Motorcycle Lie

- Donut Lab/Verge claimed **"first production motorcycle off the line"** on the last day of Q1 2026
- An **internal video** to reservation holders revealed the bikes were for **Verge's internal fleet** — i.e., pre-production vehicles for manufacturing refinement
- CEO Marko Lehtimäki later admitted on Finnish media:
  - The **400 Wh/kg cells were not in any bikes**
  - The cell VTT tested "is not even the cell that's going to be shipped to customers"
- **Leaked emails** showed Donut Lab repeatedly asking CT Coatings for proof of claims — proof never provided
